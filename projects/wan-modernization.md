# Project: Cross-Border WAN & Multi-Site Modernization

## 📋 Executive Overview
Led the complete architectural redesign and deployment of a resilient, high-availability wide-area network (WAN) spanning mission-critical remote and urban enterprise sites for the utility/telecom sector.

## ⚙️ Architecture & Technologies
* **WAN Media:** Fiber, VSAT, and Radio microwave links for robust redundancy.
* **Routing & Switching:** Enterprise-grade Cisco routing architectures.
* **Security & Perimeter:** FortiGate / Palo Alto next-gen firewalls integrated across all remote nodes.

## 🔑 Key Challenges & Solutions
* **Challenge:** High latency and single points of failure across remote field locations.
* **Solution:** Designed a hybrid mesh topology utilizing redundant satellite and fiber failover paths, reducing downtime incidents by over 99%.

## 📈 Impact & Business Value
* Guaranteed seamless real-time telemetry and operational data flow for critical infrastructure assets.
* Eliminated chronic network bottlenecks, ensuring zero-interruption uptime for core enterprise operations.
